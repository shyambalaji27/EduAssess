Read Every Question Carefully

Continuous Countdown Timer

The countdown timer commences as soon as you click "Begin Examination". The timer turns red with a pulsating warning when less than 5 minutes remain.

Your responses and remaining time are synchronized automatically. You can review and modify your answers at any point prior to final confirmation.
